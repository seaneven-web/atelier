This page warm-starts on a CIFAR-10 sample, then learns the portfolio's palette, tone and brush texture right here in the browser and paints mood pieces from them — a preview of the workflow, not the painter. The full engine (atelier.py) draws what you ask for with a text-to-image model (SD-Turbo) and repaints it in the artist's style with neural style transfer; the optional trained actor/critic adds a second opinion. The recipe it needs is exactly what the optimized-prompt card shows.
